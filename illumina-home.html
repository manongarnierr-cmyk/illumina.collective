The collective

A collective built around one light — yoga as the way back to each other.

Illumina brings people together in Barcelona to move, breathe and play — not in a studio, but outdoors, where the practice can meet the sea, the sun and each other. Every gathering pairs yoga with something else: painting, paddle, sailing, music, even pizza. The practice is the door; the community is the point.
