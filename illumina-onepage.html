illumina is a yoga collective in Barcelona — not just yoga, a space to explore, play and feel. We gather outdoors, pairing practice with painting, paddle, sailing, music, even pizza.

the collective

Come back to your light, together.

Founded on the idea that yoga is better shared, illumina brings people together across Barcelona — on rooftops, at the beach, on a paddle board, under a full moon.
