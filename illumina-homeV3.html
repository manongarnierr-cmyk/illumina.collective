illumina is a yoga collective in Barcelona — not just yoga, a space to explore, play and feel. We gather outdoors, pairing practice with painting, paddle, sailing, music, even pizza. No hierarchy, no dogma — just an open circle.

the collective

Come back to your light, together.

Founded on the idea that yoga is better shared, illumina brings people together across Barcelona — on rooftops, at the beach, on a paddle board, under a full moon. Every gathering pairs breath and movement with something unexpected.
