Our story

Illumina started with a simple observation: the best part of any yoga class wasn't the poses — it was the feeling of showing up as a stranger and leaving as part of something. Callu and Martina met practicing on a Barcelona rooftop, and quickly realised they wanted to build more of that: mornings by the sea, evenings under the full moon, a community that grows a little with every session.

What started as a handful of friends on a rooftop is now a rhythm — weekly gatherings, seasonal rituals, and an ever-growing circle of people who come back not just for the practice, but for each other. [Texte complet à venir]
